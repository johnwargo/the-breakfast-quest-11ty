The Breakfast Quest

One couple's search for great breakfast places all over the US and beyond. We love traveling and when we do, we make it a point to find the best breakfast spots. More often that not, we look for little hole in the wall, out of the way, "greasy spoon" diners. This is our blog chronicling the quest that has no end.
