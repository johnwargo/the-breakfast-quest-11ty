The Breakfast Quest

A couple's search for great breakfast places across the US and beyond.

We love traveling and when we do, we make it a point to find the best breakfast spots. More often than not, we look for little holes in the wall, out of the way, "greasy spoon" diners. This is our blog chronicling that quest that has no end.
