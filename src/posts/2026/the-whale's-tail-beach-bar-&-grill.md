Beachside bar and grill open for breakfast, lunch and dinner. Inside and outside seating with an excellent view of the Golf of Mexico.
we were there last night for a free concert by Marc Broussard.
Limited menu, but great comfort food options. I ordered the Whaler which is basically eggs, meat, starch and bread.
Service on paper plates 
slightly odd taste to their drinks, I assume because of well water. 
One interesting menu item is a crab omelette; perhaps I'll try that the next time I'm there.
