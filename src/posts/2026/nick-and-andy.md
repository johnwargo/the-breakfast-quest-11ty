I was in the Boston area for a concert (big surprise there) and staying with an old family friend in Topsfield. Early saturday morning (a little after 7 AM) we headed out to breakfast at Nick & Andy's. I love breakfast places that open early, Nick & Andy's open at 7 every day. I also love places that open only for breakfast and lunch.

![Nick and Andy's Restaurant](/images/2026/nick-and-andy-01.jpg)

We arrived to a mostly empty restaurant, with the wait staff waiting for customers. We were quickly settled in a booth with a paper menu. The menu is big, with a lot of choices, mostly standard breakfast fare (my favorite). 

My friend eats here frequently, especially with his teenage son, so he raved about the pancakes. I'm a huge fan of pancakes, but very few places get it right. My favorite is Denny's, they're like a thick crepe and soak up the syrup. 

Anyway, no country fried steak on the menu, so I went with the pancake meal with eggs and linked sausage.

![Me after my food was served](/images/2026/nick-and-andy-02.jpg)

The buttermilk pancakes are huge, so don't order more than one unless you're very, very hungry. The eggs and sausage were very good, cooked just like I like them. 

![Eggs and sausage](/images/2026/nick-and-andy-03.jpg)

They're huge, so don't order more than one unless you're very, very hungry. The pancakes were OK. I found that I struggled to cut them, the knife wasn't sharp enough to do it (surprisingly). They soaked up the syrup like I like, but they were still pretty dense and heavy.

![a single pancake](/images/2026/nick-and-andy-04.jpg)

Buttermilk pancakes by their very nature are supposed to be light and fluffy. The baking soda and buttermilk are supposed to generate fluffiness (air pockets?) in the batter. I truly don't understand why they have to be thick and heavy and fall apart after you cut them.
