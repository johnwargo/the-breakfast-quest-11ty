Early sunday morning in Cambridge, I started looking for breakfast places. Google search had a really hard time finding me a place that offered breakfast (wasn't a coffee shop) and was open. Most places opened at 8 or 9 but I was hungry at 7. My friend suggested Little Luna cafe and we headed over there. 

Little Luna is essentially a bakery and coffee shop with a kitchen. I ordered the Kitchen Sink, which is, as you can see from the image below, eggs, bacon and sausage, potatoes and french toast. 

![My bacon and egg breakfast](/images/2026/little-luna-01.jpg)

The eggs were cooked just like I like them, over easy. The bacon? It really felt microwaved to me. Nice and thick, but clearly not cooked on a grill. The potatoes were dark brown like they were heavily seasoned and crispy but they simply weren't. The sausage patty was well cooked, I guess, but likely microwaved. 

I had high expectations for the french toast, but it was rock hard and hard to cut. It tasted OK, but definitely not very good.

This is an OK breakfast place in a pinch, but I know I wouldn't go again unless there wasn't any other option.
